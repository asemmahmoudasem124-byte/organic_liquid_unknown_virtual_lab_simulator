Scheme for Identification of Organic Liquid Unknown

Ethanol, Methanol, Glycerol, Formalin, Acetaldehyde, Acetone, Formic acid, Acetic acid, Ethyl acetate

1- Miscibility Test (1 mL Unknown + 2 mL H₂O) & Litmus Paper (L.P.)

* Miscible with H₂O, L.P. → Neutral

∴ May be: Ethanol, Methanol, Glycerol, Acetaldehyde, Acetone, or Formalin

N.B. Trap: Acetaldehyde & Formalin may give slightly acidic L.P., but Na₂CO₃ test → No eff. (weak acid) → treat as neutral!
** Miscible with H₂O, L.P. → Acidic

Na₂CO₃ test → Effervescence (Strong acid)

∴ May be: Formic acid or Acetic acid

*** Immiscible with H₂O

Lighter than H₂O (forms upper oily layer)

∴ Ethyl acetate

2- Iodoform Test (Neutral Group)

1 mL unknown + 3 mL 10% NaOH + excess I₂ (till persistent yellow) + warm between hands.

Yellow ppt.:

Ethanol, Acetaldehyde, or Acetone

3- Na Nitroprusside Test:

1mL unk + 3mL 30% NaOH + 2mL NP

  • • No red color → Ethanol
  • • Red color → Acetaldehyde or Acetone:
    4- 30% NaOH (boiling):
    - Yellow resin → Acetaldehyde
    - No yellow resin → Acetone (Confirm: NaHSO₃)
No yellow ppt.:

Formalin, Methanol, or Glycerol

3- Salicylic Acid Test (Dry T.T.):

1 spat Salicylic + 3-4 drops unk + 5 drops Conc. H₂SO₄ on wall (Don't shake!)

  • • +ve (Crimson red) → Formalin (Confirm: Cu-mirror)
  • • -ve → Methanol or Glycerol:
    4- Borax Test:
    - Pink disappears on cold & reappears on heat → Glycerol
    - Stays pink (-ve) → Methanol (Confirm: Esterification)

2- Alkaline KMnO₄ Test (Formic vs Acetic Acid)

3 mL KMnO₄ + 2 spatulas Na₂CO₃ + 1–2 drops unknown.

Decolorization & Brown ppt.:

∴ Formic acid

Confirm with HgCl₂ test (1mL acid + 1mL HgCl₂, boil on direct flame → white ppt turning grey).

No Decolorization & No Brown ppt.:

∴ Acetic acid

Confirm with HgCl₂ test (No precipitate on boiling).
